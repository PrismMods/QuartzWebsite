# Tile Arc

Rounds the outer corner of every tile turn. A Dance of Fire and Ice only rounds turns inside a narrow angle band and draws every other turn as a sharp point; Tile Arc gives every turn the same rounded corner, and lets you choose how round.

## Where to find it

Open the Quartz menu in-game and pick **Visuals → Tile Arc** from the sidebar. Turn on **Use this feature** at the top of the page to apply it — tiles already on screen update straight away.

## Settings

- **Arc Intensity** — how far the rounded corner reaches, as a percentage of the tile's width. 100% is a full half-circle corner; 0% leaves the turn sharp. Moving the slider re-shapes the tiles in view live.
